About me

My name is Vương Anh Vũ, it's a Vietnamese name. I am an engineer with strong English skills, holding an IELTS score of 8.0. My fascination lies in how computers transform ideas into reality, which inspired me to pursue this very web development career. With unprecedented speed of deployment thanks to AI tools, in combination with my technical skills and my understanding of current trends and clear vision for an uncertain future, I want to help people publish their ideas to the world and contribute to moving society forward through technology. Alongside my technical journey, I am also an English teacher. I believe English is the language of today’s technology-driven world, and I am passionate about guiding the younger generation in Vietnam to master it for a brighter future. Teaching allows me to empower students with the tools they need to connect globally and unlock opportunities. Beyond work, I enjoy playing tennis, which keeps me active and competitive, and I find peace in nature, which balances my life. Together, these passions shape who I am: a developer, a teacher, and a lifelong learner.
